About CrowdPass

Ticketing built for
every kind of event

We're a UK-based ticketing platform giving organisers the tools to sell tickets simply — from a small local fundraiser to a full-scale concert with seat maps and branded subdomains.

Our mission

Ticketing shouldn't be complicated or expensive. We built CrowdPass because organisers were paying too much to platforms that weren't built for their needs — and buyers were getting a poor experience as a result.

We offer two paths: a self-serve option for anyone who wants to list an event quickly, and a fully managed bespoke service for organisers who need seat maps, custom branding and dedicated support. Both are built on the same platform, the same infrastructure, the same commitment to reliability.

What we offer

Self-serve events

Create and publish an event in minutes. Set your own ticket types, pricing and capacity. We charge a small fee per ticket sold — you pay nothing upfront.

Bespoke events

For concerts, venues and large-scale shows. We build a fully branded page on your own subdomain, complete with an interactive seat map, admin dashboard and dedicated account support.

Secure by default

All payments are processed through certified payment providers. QR-code tickets prevent fraud. Attendee data is handled in line with UK GDPR.
